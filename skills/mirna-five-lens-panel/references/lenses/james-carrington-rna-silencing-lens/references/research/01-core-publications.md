## Recurring scientific patterns supported across contexts

1. **Disaggregate virus–host interactions.** The record repeatedly separates viral suppressor activity, host pathway components, small-RNA outputs, tissue context and disease phenotype (`claim-carrington-a-001`, `002`, `007`, `010`).
2. **Resolve paralogs genetically and combinatorially.** Single and higher-order mutant comparisons reveal specialization, redundancy and context dependence among DCL, RDR and AGO family members (`003`, `007`, `009`, `010`).
3. **Treat secondary-siRNA entry as context dependent.** Cleavage position, small-RNA length, AGO identity, target-site architecture and RDR/DCL evidence jointly constrain interpretation (`004`–`006`, `008`).
4. **Pair molecular and phenotypic readouts.** Small-RNA or virus measurements and organismal traits answer different causal questions (`007`, `009`, `010`).
5. **Preserve the model-to-crop transfer boundary.** Recent crop studies extend the research program, but their correlation and engineering results remain conditional rather than universal (`011`, `012`).

## miRNA-specific interpretation guardrails

- A miRNA-guided cleavage event is not itself proof of secondary-siRNA production, target-mediated phenotype or complete causal mechanism.
- miRNA length, AGO partner, target-site architecture and transcript context must remain explicit; animal seed heuristics are not substituted for plant complementarity and experimental evidence.
- Mature miRNA, MIR locus, precursor, 5p/3p product, isomiR and derived phasiRNA/tasiRNA are not interchangeable entities.
- A viral small-RNA profile is evidence about biogenesis/output, not by itself evidence for complete antiviral phenotype causality.
