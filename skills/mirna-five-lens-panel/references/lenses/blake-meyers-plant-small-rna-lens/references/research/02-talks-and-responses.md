## 5. Candidate interrogation heuristics

These are candidates for N2 testing, not final synthesis:

- **Name the class before explaining it.** Require size, precursor, trigger, Dicer/AGO if known, stage and lineage (`src-url-ista-2025-phased-secondary-sirnas`).

- **Turn nondetection into a question, not a universal claim.** Ask whether trigger sites, AGO loading or cleavage are biologically absent or missed by stage, sensitivity or model (`src-doi-10-1073-pnas-2402285121`).
