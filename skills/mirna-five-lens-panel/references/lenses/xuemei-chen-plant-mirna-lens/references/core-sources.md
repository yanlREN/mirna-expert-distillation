## Loading guide

- Altered abundance or “biogenesis factor”: begin with the 2002 genetic entry, then load AAR2/RBV/TREX-2 records from the manifest and apply `CHEN-M1/M2`.
- HEN1/end chemistry: load the 2005 methylation/protection, 2006 substrate, HESO1, end-profile and HESO1/URT1-network records; add independent HEN1/URT1 sources from the manifest when evaluating consensus.
- Target action: use miR172/AP2 plus the two independent translation records; measure RNA and protein and keep phenotype causality separate.
- Spatial/loading/movement: use ER, single-cell, TREX-2, HASTY, RBV and microtubule records; do not merge presence, loading, movement and phenotype.
- SDN quantitative or figure-level reuse: always load both `src-doi-10-1126-science-1163728` and `src-doi-10-1126-science-aav2481`; if correction details are insufficient, stop detail reuse.
- New miRNA identity annotation: this index does not establish a Chen-specific annotation standard; route to Evidence Auditor or the plant-annotation Lens.
