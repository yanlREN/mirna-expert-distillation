## Standing synthesis

### Broadly supported, with scope limits

- Cross-organism RNA effects are biologically plausible and causal in some plant–fungus systems, but direction, RNA class, uptake competence and target architecture must be specified (`HJ-B-C005`, `HJ-C-C008`).
- Extracellular plant preparations contain RNA and EV populations are heterogeneous; extracellular abundance does not establish vesicle encapsulation or function (`HJ-B-C007`, `HJ-B-C008`, `HJ-C-C013`).
- SIGS/HIGS can reduce disease in controlled Botrytis and Fusarium systems, while route, persistence and species response vary (`HJ-B-C012`, `HJ-C-C015`, `HJ-C-C016`).
- Detection, release/vehicle, uptake, effector access, direct target and phenotype causality are experimentally separable (`HJ-B-C002`, `HJ-C-C004`).

### Expert-program and team-position evidence

- Jin-authored reviews/perspectives recurrently frame cross-kingdom RNA traffic as bidirectional and environmental RNAi as a promising intervention (`HJ-C-C001`, `HJ-C-C002`, `HJ-C-C003`).
- Team studies provide detailed positive mechanisms in Arabidopsis–Botrytis and application-oriented delivery systems (`HJ-A-C009`, `HJ-A-C010`, `HJ-A-C014`, `HJ-A-C018`).
- These are public team/project patterns, not claims about a private or current individual opinion.

### Contested or context-dependent

- The importance of fungal sRNAs for Botrytis virulence differs across host, stage, strain and assayed sRNA class (`HJ-A-C006`, `HJ-C-C011`).
- Plant EV sRNA vehicle and cargo composition remain sensitive to preparation, subclass, topology and non-EV comparators (`HJ-A-C009`, `HJ-B-C008`, `HJ-C-C013`, `HJ-C-C014`).
- Cross-kingdom RNAi and external-RNA uptake are not universal across fungi, as the wheat–Zymoseptoria negatives show (`HJ-C-C009`, `HJ-C-C010`).
- Controlled RNA protection is pre-field evidence and cannot establish environmental persistence, non-target safety or deployment maturity (`HJ-C-C003`, `HJ-B-C012`).

## Methodological tensions

### Tension T1 — Botrytis fungal-sRNA virulence: positive mechanism versus bounded non-replication

`HJ-A-S006` reports selected Botrytis sRNAs, host AGO1 engagement, target repression and virulence-related genetics (`HJ-A-C006`). `HJ-C-S012` reports that deleting a major transposon region or both fungal DCLs reduced transposon-derived sRNAs by more than 99% without reducing virulence during early tomato infection (`HJ-C-C011`).

**Preserved boundary:** Arabidopsis versus tomato, infection timing, strain background, sRNA source class, target set and AGO assay differ. The external result is a meaningful partial non-replication, not a universal refutation; the original positive is not allowed to override it. Resolution needs matched strain×host reciprocal experiments, time-resolved recipient AGO capture, exact target-site genetics, complementation and biomass/mixed-cell controls.

### Tension T2 — EV-associated selected guides versus tiny-RNA-rich and non-EV extracellular pools

Jin-team TET8-positive studies support selected sRNAs and candidate RBP contributions (`HJ-A-C009`, `HJ-A-C010`). Independent Arabidopsis work finds strong 10–17 nt enrichment in operational EV preparations and distinct abundant leaf-surface RNA pools (`HJ-B-C008`, `HJ-C-C013`). The human EV analogue further warns that RNA/RBP may reside in non-vesicular fractions (`HJ-C-C014`).

**Preserved boundary:** infection state, starting material, EV subclass, density/immunocapture, library size selection and lineage differ. Needed resolution includes harmonized isolation, spike-ins, nuclease ± detergent, TET8/PEN1 subclass capture, absolute RNA quantification and recipient-cell delivery assays.

### Tension T3 — Cross-kingdom RNAi feasibility versus recipient-incompetent pathosystems

Cotton–Verticillium and Botrytis/Fusarium systems support selected natural or engineered RNA effects (`HJ-C-C008`, `HJ-C-C015`). Two independent wheat–Zymoseptoria networks find no clear uptake, virulence dependency or cleavage evidence in tested settings (`HJ-C-C009`, `HJ-C-C010`).

**Preserved boundary:** organism-pair, uptake competence, DCL/AGO state, stage and assay differ. Positive systems establish feasibility; Zymoseptoria establishes a strong negative boundary for the tested contexts. PARE nondetection constrains cleavage but not every hypothetical non-cleavage event.

### Tension T4 — Direct pathogen uptake versus plant-passage routes in SIGS

Botrytis work supports direct fungal uptake under tested conditions (`HJ-A-C008`), while a Fusarium–barley study supports plant passage and recipient DCL1 dependence (`HJ-C-C015`). Another Fusarium study reports transient, wound-sensitive and species-variable effects (`HJ-C-C016`).

**Preserved boundary:** route is an empirical variable, not a universal property. Every new crop–pathogen system needs direct-versus-plant-passage comparisons, intact-surface controls, uptake/processing measures and time courses.

### Tension T5 — EV loading versus stabilization versus recovery

RBP perturbations change selected sRNA abundance in TET8-positive fractions (`HJ-B-C009`), but such changes can arise from loading, stabilization, production, EV number, localization or recovery efficiency (`HJ-C-C006`).

**Preserved boundary:** use kinetics, topology, total/extracellular/EV normalization and EV-number controls. Do not compress association, loading and stabilization into one mechanism.

### Tension T6 — Application promise versus field and ecological maturity

The public perspective is explicitly optimistic about environmental RNAi (`HJ-C-C003`), and controlled studies demonstrate protection or longer persistence (`HJ-A-C012`, `HJ-A-C013`, `HJ-C-C015`). Yet wounding, transient effects, pathogen selectivity and lack of multi-environment field evidence constrain deployment (`HJ-C-C016`).

**Preserved boundary:** optimism is an `expert_position`; field readiness requires dose, rainfastness, UV/temperature exposure, intact plants, multi-location/multi-season trials, non-targets, off-targets, resistance evolution, production and regulatory evidence.

## Honest conclusion

The defensible synthesis is neither “cross-kingdom RNAi is universally established” nor “it has been disproved.” The record supports a direction-resolved, vehicle-aware, recipient-competence-gated and context-specific framework. Negative evidence narrows pathosystem scope; positive evidence establishes bounded feasibility; neither permits untested transport, target, phenotype or field edges to be filled by reputation or repetition.
